---
title: "What to say"
description: "The phrases Dave understands"
---

Type these in a direct message with Dave. Words in [brackets] are slots: replace them with a deal or a rep name. Type `help` in Slack for the same list.

## Ask me

| Say | Dave does |
|---|---|
| `what's on my plate` | Lists your open tasks, soonest first |
| `anything I missed` | Shows deals past close, gone quiet, or slipping |
| `what's closing soon` | Shows deals landing in the next two weeks |
| `close date on [deal]` | Finds the deal and reads the date back |

## Ask about the team

Dave can read every rep's deals, tasks and activity. He does not read email bodies.

| Say | Dave does |
|---|---|
| `who's top this quarter` | Shows revenue won, deals and win rate by rep |
| `[rep]'s tasks` | Shows anyone's queue, not only yours |
| `what has [rep] done today` | Shows their meetings, calls and notes |
| `full performance of [rep]` | Shows revenue, pipeline and activity together |

## Change something

Dave changes only deals that you own. He shows each change first. See [Safety rules](/safety).

| Say | Dave does |
|---|---|
| `push [deal] two weeks` | Moves a close date |
| `move [deal] to [stage]` | Changes the stage |
| `note on [deal]: budget approved` | Logs what happened |
| `task: call [deal] Friday` | Creates a task |
| `mark that task done` | Closes a task |

## Watch a deal

| Say | Dave does |
|---|---|
| `watch this` + a HubSpot deal link | Tells you when that deal changes |
| `stop watching` | Stops |

## Turn Dave down

| Say | Dave does |
|---|---|
| `quiet` | Sends nothing unprompted for a week |
| `nudge me twice a day` | Sets how often Dave starts a conversation |
| `settings` | Shows your settings with a button to change them |

<Note>
  Dave uses your own Slack quiet hours. He does not keep a second copy of them.
</Note>
