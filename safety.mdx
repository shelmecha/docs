---
title: "Safety rules"
description: "Who can change what"
---

Dave reads widely and writes narrowly.

## Reading

- Dave can read deals, tasks and activity for any rep. This is by design.
- Dave talks only to the people on the ConstructEdge list. A stranger who messages Dave gets one line that says who to ask.
- Dave does not fetch email bodies. He reads email subjects only.

## Writing

1. **Your deals only.** Dave changes a deal only if you own it. An unassigned deal is read-only.
2. **You confirm.** Dave shows the change first, for example `Close date Sat 5 Dec -> Sat 12 Dec`. Nothing is saved until you press **Confirm**.
3. **Buttons take two taps.** The first tap shows the exact change. The second tap saves the date that you saw.
4. **The Update a deal form saves on Save.** You change a field that you can see, so there is no second review step.
5. **A master switch.** HubSpot writes can be switched off for the whole app. When off, no change can leave Dave.

<Warning>
  A model never holds a write tool. The model proposes. Dave's code writes.
</Warning>

## Unprompted messages

- Dave starts a conversation only inside your settings and your Slack quiet hours.
- A failed check is silence. Dave does not send "I could not check your pipeline".
- A watched deal reports when it changes, not on a schedule.
