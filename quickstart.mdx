## Before you start

- You are a ConstructEdge sales rep in the Slack workspace where Dave is installed.
- Your Slack user is linked to your HubSpot owner record. Ask Shelvi if Dave says he does not know you.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a direct message with Dave">
    Find **Agent Dave** in Slack and open a direct message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for help">
    Type `help`. Dave shows the full list of what he can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Type `what's on my plate`. Dave lists your open tasks, soonest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a change">
    Type `push [deal] two weeks`. Dave shows the new close date. Press **Confirm** to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Type `settings` to change how often Dave messages you, or to switch him off for a week with `quiet`.
</Tip>
