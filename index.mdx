Agent Dave is a Slack assistant for ConstructEdge sales reps. He reads your HubSpot deals, tasks and activity. He tells you when a deal needs attention. He changes a deal only after you confirm.

## What Dave does

- **Answers questions.** Ask about your tasks, your deals and the team's numbers in plain words.
- **Speaks first.** Dave sends a message when a deal is past its close date, has gone quiet, or is slipping. You set how often.
- **Changes deals.** Move a close date or a stage, add a note, or create a task. Dave shows the change first. You press Confirm.
- **Watches a deal.** Paste a HubSpot deal link and say "watch this". Dave tells you when that deal changes.

<Warning>
  Dave changes only deals that you own. A change never happens without your Confirm or Save.
</Warning>
